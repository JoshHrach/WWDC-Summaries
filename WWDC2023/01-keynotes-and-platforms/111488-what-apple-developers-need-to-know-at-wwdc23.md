**WWDC23 · Session 111488** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/111488/)

_Platforms:_ iOS 17, iPadOS 17, macOS Sonoma 14, tvOS 17, watchOS 10, visionOS 1

## Overview
This is a brief promotional overview video introducing WWDC23 to Apple developers. It highlights the breadth of the conference — over 175 new instructional sessions from hundreds of Apple engineers and designers — covering all Apple platforms including the all-new visionOS.

The video teases major themes of WWDC23: SwiftData, widgets expansion, watchOS 10 design, SwiftUI custom animations, Xcode improvements (enhanced test navigator, previews, DocC documentation), AAA games on Apple silicon (Mac Pro, Mac Studio with M2), and visionOS/Reality Composer Pro development. It directs developers to developer.apple.com for the full Platform State of the Union and session catalog.

This session contains no technical content, code samples, or API introductions; it serves purely as an orientation and promotional entry point for the conference.
