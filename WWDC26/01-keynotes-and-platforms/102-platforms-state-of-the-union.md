## Overview
The Platforms State of the Union is the essential developer follow-up to the WWDC26 Keynote. Where the Keynote focuses on user-facing features, this session goes deeper on the newest advancements across Apple platforms from a developer's perspective — covering APIs, frameworks, tools, and the technologies developers need to know to build great apps this year.

This session is traditionally the highest-value watch for engineers, as it synthesizes the breadth of new platform capabilities into a coherent picture of what to prioritize and where to invest.
