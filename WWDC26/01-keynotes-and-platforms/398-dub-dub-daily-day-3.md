## Overview
Dub Dub Daily: Day 3 is the mid-week episode in Apple's daily WWDC26 recap series. Day 3 is dedicated to Apple Intelligence — covering the latest AI announcements in depth, examining what they mean for app developers, and exploring the new possibilities that weren't available before. This episode is specifically valuable for developers building apps that leverage Apple Intelligence, on-device machine learning, or any AI-powered features.
