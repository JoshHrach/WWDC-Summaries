# WWDC22 Day 3 recap
**WWDC22 · Session 110931** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/110931/)

_Platforms:_ iOS 16, iPadOS 16, macOS Ventura 13

## Overview
A short (~1.5 minute) highlight reel of Day 3 sessions at WWDC22. The clip recaps Wednesday sessions and teases what is coming on Day 4 (Thursday). Highlights include: designing App Shortcuts for the new App Intents framework; building desktop-class iPad apps with the new UIKit navigation APIs and toolbar customization; advanced iOS camera capture with depth, focus, and multitasking; SwiftUI custom layout compositions (the `Layout` protocol and `Grid`); ARKit 6 advances; Metal mesh shaders; watchOS WidgetKit complications; and hang detection with Xcode.

## Key Topics

### App Shortcuts Design
Session 10169 "Design App Shortcuts" covers best practices for surfacing app functionality through Siri and the Shortcuts app using the new App Intents framework.

### Desktop-class iPad Apps
Session 10070 "Build a desktop-class iPad app" covers UIKit navigation bar customization, title menus, center item groups, and other iPad-specific UI patterns for creating pro-level iPad workflows.

### iOS Camera Capture
Session 110429 covers depth capture, focus lock, and multitasking-aware camera sessions in the latest iOS camera APIs.

### SwiftUI Custom Layouts
Sessions 10056 "Compose custom layouts with SwiftUI" covers the new `Layout` protocol, `Grid`, `GridRow`, and `AnyLayout` for building custom view arrangements.

### ARKit 6
Session 10126 "Discover ARKit 6" covers 4K video capture in AR sessions, improved motion capture, plane detection, and other ARKit advances.

### Metal Mesh Shaders
Session 10162 "Transform your geometry with Metal mesh shaders" covers the new Metal mesh shading pipeline for GPU-driven geometry generation.
