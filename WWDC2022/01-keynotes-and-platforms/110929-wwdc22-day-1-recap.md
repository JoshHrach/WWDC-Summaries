# WWDC22 Day 1 recap
**WWDC22 · Session 110929** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/110929/)

_Platforms:_ iOS 16, iPadOS 16, macOS Ventura 13, watchOS 9

## Overview
A short (~2 minute) highlight reel of Day 1 announcements at WWDC22, presented by Serenity Caldwell. The clip covers the major consumer and developer announcements from the opening Keynote and Platforms State of the Union: iOS 16's reimagined Lock Screen with fonts, colors, and widgets; Focus modes; message editing/unsending; SharePlay in Messages; iCloud Shared Photo Library; CarPlay previews; Maps multi-stop routing (up to 15 stops); watchOS 9 new workout views, medication reminders, and watch faces; and the introduction of the M2 chip powering the redesigned MacBook Air and updated 13-inch MacBook Pro.

The recap also highlights developer platform advances announced: WeatherKit, Metal 3, SwiftUI enhancements, Swift Charts, Swift Regex, Xcode Cloud opening to all developers, Stage Manager for macOS Ventura and iPadOS 16, enhanced Spotlight image search, Continuity Camera, and the 12 Apple Design Award winners.

## Key Topics

### iOS 16
Redesigned Lock Screen with customizable fonts, colors, and widget slots; Personal Focus linking Lock Screen styles to Focus modes; message editing and unsending in Messages; SharePlay in Messages for synchronized media viewing; photo subject lift (tap to isolate subject from background); iCloud Shared Photo Library for family sharing.

### macOS Ventura
Stage Manager for window and app organization; enhanced Spotlight with image search; email Schedule Send and Undo Send; Continuity Camera (iPhone as Mac webcam); M2 in MacBook Air (redesigned, thinner, lighter) and 13-inch MacBook Pro.

### iPadOS 16
Stage Manager with multi-window support; cross-app and cross-device collaboration tools; new live collaboration app (coming later that year).

### watchOS 9
Heart rate zone workout views; custom workout creation; medication reminders; new watch faces: Astronomy, Lunar, Playtime, Metropolitan, Buddy.

### Developer Platforms
M2 chip introduction; WeatherKit API; Metal 3; SwiftUI new features; Swift Charts; Swift Regex; Xcode Cloud open to all developers; Apple Design Awards for 12 winning apps/games.
