# Keynote
**WWDC26 · Session 101** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/101/)

_Platforms:_ All platforms (iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, tvOS 27, visionOS 27)

## Overview
The WWDC26 Keynote is Apple's flagship annual reveal of the latest software and technologies across all of its platforms. The presentation covers major platform improvements, new trust and safety initiatives, and a significant expansion of Apple Intelligence and Siri capabilities.

The Keynote is the primary event for developers and consumers alike to learn what's coming in the next generation of Apple operating systems. It sets the strategic direction for the year and signals which technologies Apple is investing in most heavily.

This year's Keynote is organized around three major themes: platform improvements to core OS features, a new generation of trust and safety protections, and a transformative leap forward for Apple Intelligence and Siri.

## Key Topics

**Platform Improvements** (0:05:04)
Apple highlighted broad improvements across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, and visionOS. This chapter covers quality-of-life enhancements and foundational changes developers will need to account for when updating their apps.

**Trust and Safety** (0:16:56)
A dedicated section on new features and frameworks designed to protect users from scams, coercion, and privacy threats. This reflects Apple's continued investment in building safety directly into the platform layer, including features tied to the new Trust Insights framework announced at WWDC26.

**Apple Intelligence and Siri** (0:27:53)
The largest chapter of the Keynote covers the next evolution of Apple Intelligence. Siri receives a major upgrade in iOS 27, gaining more powerful natural language abilities, deeper on-device AI capabilities, and tighter integration across the system. Image generation through Image Playground is enhanced with photorealistic output, and Siri AI gains the ability to edit and compose emails, texts, and documents in a more conversational way.
