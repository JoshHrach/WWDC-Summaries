## Overview
Dub Dub Daily: Day 2 is a daily recap show produced by Apple for WWDC26, consolidating the biggest announcements of the conference into a single watch. Day 2 focuses on the major reveals from the opening day of WWDC26 — bringing together everything announced, placing it in thematic context, and featuring a special guest to help break it all down.

The "Dub Dub Daily" series serves as a curated news digest for the conference, making it easy for developers to stay current even if they can't watch every individual session. Each episode covers the key announcements of the day, highlights the most important sessions to watch, and provides commentary on the themes shaping the conference.

Day 2 covers the aftermath of the Keynote and Platforms State of the Union, giving viewers deeper context on the year's most important announcements and themes.
