# Get Ready for WWDC26
**WWDC26 · Session 394** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/394/)

_Platforms:_ All platforms

## Overview
This orientation session gives developers the inside track on how to get the most out of WWDC26 — covering how to navigate sessions, labs, and every opportunity Apple offers during the conference week. It is the recommended first watch before diving into technical content.

The session explains the structure of WWDC26, introduces the types of resources available (video sessions, Group Labs, one-on-one consultations), and provides practical guidance on planning a productive week. It is particularly valuable for developers attending WWDC for the first time or those who want to make sure they don't miss important opportunities like lab appointments.

A dedicated resource link is provided to sign up for Group Labs directly from the session page.

## Key Topics

**Navigating WWDC26**
An overview of how the conference is structured in 2026, including the full catalog of developer sessions available online, live and on-demand formats, and how to find content relevant to your specific development focus.

**Sessions**
Guidance on how to get the most out of technical sessions — including how to search for topics, use chapters to jump to relevant parts of a video, and identify the sessions most relevant to your project.

**Labs**
Explanation of the two types of labs available at WWDC26:
- **Group Labs** — facilitated group sessions where developers can ask Apple engineers questions on specific topics. Sign-up link provided on the session page.
- **One-on-one consultations** — direct time with Apple engineers to get feedback on your specific app or technical challenge.

**Making the Most of Every Moment**
Tips for prioritizing content, connecting with the developer community, using the Apple Developer app and developer forums, and following up on sessions after the conference.

## Takeaways
- Watch this session before starting the WWDC26 technical content to orient yourself to the conference structure.
- Sign up for Group Labs early — spots fill up quickly. Use the link at [developer.apple.com/wwdc26/schedule/group-labs](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc26/schedule/group-labs).
- Use the Apple Developer app to build a watch list and track sessions across the week.
- Labs are one of the most valuable WWDC resources — come with specific questions and code to show Apple engineers.
