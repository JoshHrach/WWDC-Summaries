# Out of this world... on to Mars
**WWDC21 · Session 10317** · [Watch](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10317/)

_Platforms:_ N/A (inspirational keynote talk)

## Overview
Tiera Fletcher, a rocket scientist working on NASA's Space Launch System (SLS), shares her journey from a small town outside Atlanta to becoming a structural design and analysis engineer at Boeing/NASA at age 22. Her talk traces humanity's ambitions in space exploration — from the Saturn V to Voyager probes, OSIRIS-REx, New Horizons, and the Perseverance Mars Rover.

Fletcher describes what it means to design and verify structural components of the most powerful rocket ever built, standing 322 feet tall and producing over 8.8 million pounds of thrust. She walks through the Artemis program's three missions planned to return humans to the Moon and set the stage for crewed missions to Mars.

A central theme is diversity and representation in STEM. Fletcher reflects on being the youngest, the only woman, and the only African American on her teams, and how she co-founded "Rocket with the Fletchers" to inspire the next generation of aerospace engineers through outreach, mentorship, and advocacy for diversity of mind in innovation.

## Key Topics
- **Personal Journey**: From childhood passion for math and engineering to MIT to NASA/Boeing SLS team
- **NASA Space Launch System (SLS)**: Architecture of the 322-foot rocket — RS-25 engines, core stage, interim cryogenic propulsion stage (ICPS), solid rocket boosters, and the Orion crew capsule
- **Green Run Testing**: Simultaneous firing of all four RS-25 engines at Stennis Space Center
- **Artemis Program**:
  - Artemis 1 (2021): Uncrewed flight, Orion travels 280,000 miles from Earth
  - Artemis 2 (2022): Four astronauts return to lunar environment for first time in 50+ years
  - Artemis 3 (2024): First woman and first person of color to walk on the Moon
- **Space Exploration Milestones**: Saturn V, Voyager 1 & 2, OSIRIS-REx, New Horizons, Perseverance Rover, Ingenuity helicopter
- **Representation & Outreach**: Mae Jemison as role model; co-founding "Rocket with the Fletchers"

## Takeaways
- Tiera Fletcher exemplifies how passion, persistence, and representation can drive extraordinary careers in STEM fields.
- The NASA Space Launch System is the most powerful rocket ever built, producing 15% more thrust than the Saturn V, and is the vehicle intended to take humans to the Moon (Artemis) and eventually Mars.
- Diversity of mind is positioned as essential to innovation — broadening who participates in space exploration leads to better outcomes for all of humanity.
- Developers and engineers across all fields can draw parallels: seeing designs go from code/drawings to real-world products is a universal engineering thrill.
